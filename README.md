
# Ranko 🎯

**Ranko is AI SEO and content software that creates content built to rank on Google *and* get cited by AI answer engines** — so you win search today and tomorrow.

Buyers don't just Google anymore. They ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini what to choose. Ranko shows you how often AI mentions, links, and ranks your brand against competitors — with every number backed by the real AI answer behind it.

## Why Ranko?

Most teams still respond to ranking drops reactively — someone opens a spreadsheet, compares manually, and loses hours before acting.  Ranko skips that step. It detects the drop, diagnoses the cause, and prescribes the fix — automatically.

And it's not just about blue links anymore. A page ranking position 10 that gets cited in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers is outperforming a position-3 page AI assistants ignore completely.  Ranko measures both.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Engine AI Citation Tracking** — Daily monitoring of mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Claude,  so you know exactly when and how AI assistants cite your content.
- **Real-Time Rank Tracking** — Tracks every mention, link, and ranking, showing your share of voice against competitors. 
- **Automatic Gap Detection** — Where competitors show up and you don't, Ranko suggests a fix, assigns an owner, and tracks results. 
- **Change Attribution** — Identifies *why* a ranking moved: algorithm update, competitor content change, backlink shift, or on-page drift — not just that it moved.
- **CITE Score Auditing** — Scores pages across five dimensions — citability, indexability, topical authority, evidence density, and structured markup — the exact factors AI models weigh before citing a page. 
- **Page Refresher** — Scores existing pages against 18 AI citation criteria and surfaces side-by-side rewrites  to close visibility gaps fast.
- **Topic Planner** — Builds 90-day publishing plans directly from real questions your buyers ask on Google and AI assistants.
- **Full Transparency** — Every AI response is saved exactly as written, with a timestamp — click any number to see the question, the tool, and the full answer behind it.

## How It Works

1. **Add Your Questions & Competitors** — List the questions your buyers actually ask AI (e.g. "best CRM for real estate teams") and add your competitors.
2. **Ranko Checks Every AI Engine** — Each question runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and more, on a weekly or daily schedule. 
3. **See Where You Stand** — Every screen starts with your current position — clear numbers, not raw data dumps.
4. **Fix the Gaps** — When competitors are cited and you're not, Ranko turns that gap into a content brief, built from the exact questions you're losing.
